# 3D Printed Fidget Toy Design Portfolio: 5 Unique Prototypes

This portfolio presents 5 original mechanical fidget toy concepts inspired by the modular, 100% snap-fit, support-free design language of the **Fidget-Fuse Tactical**. Each prototype draws inspiration from a distinct real-world mechanical object and incorporates at least **5 distinct tactile fidget interactions**.

---

## ✈️ Prototype 1: "Aero-Throttle"
**Inspiration:** Fighter Jet Cockpit Flight Stick (HOTAS) & Avionics Console

![Aero-Throttle Prototype Sketch](C:/Users/chuda/.gemini/antigravity-ide/brain/5324f30b-bc16-4635-a890-31f0949b9622/prototype1_aero_throttle_1787157922526.jpg)

### 🎯 5+ Tactile Features
1. **Linear Throttle Slider with Afterburner Gate:** Push/pull rail with a compliant leaf spring detent at 85% travel requiring a firm press to engage "afterburner".
2. **4-Way Tactile Thumb Hat Switch:** Omnidirectional joystick head resting on 4 internal radial leaf springs for crisp cardinal clicks.
3. **High-Friction Side Rotary Trim Wheel:** Knurled thumb wheel engaging an internal cantilever ratchet arm for fast acoustic clicks.
4. **Flip-Up Missile Switch Spring Guard:** Bi-stable spring cover that snaps securely into 90° open and closed positions.
5. **Dual-Stage Index Finger Trigger:** Two-step compliant trigger with soft pre-travel followed by a sharp mechanical break.
6. **Ergonomic Avionics Grip Profile:** Textured side-ribbed panels contoured to the palm.

## 📷 Prototype 2: "Retro-Lens"
**Inspiration:** Vintage 35mm Rangefinder Camera & Manual Focus Lens Barrel

![Retro-Lens Prototype Sketch](C:/Users/chuda/.gemini/antigravity-ide/brain/5324f30b-bc16-4635-a890-31f0949b9622/prototype2_retro_lens_1787158022565.jpg)

### 🎯 5+ Tactile Features
1. **12-Click Positive Aperture Ring:** Stepped rotating dial with spring-loaded ball detents simulating manual f-stops (f/1.4 to f/22).
2. **Smooth Helicoid Focus Ring:** Internal spiral multi-lead cam giving smooth, dampened, continuous rotary glide.
3. **Spring-Loaded Film Advance Flick Lever:** 60° throw thumb lever with progressive ratchet teeth and automatic spring-loaded snap return.
4. **Two-Stage Mechanical Shutter Plunger:** Top shutter button with internal tactile dome clicking spring.
5. **Diopter Slider Switch:** 3-position linear slide toggle (Left, Center, Right).
6. **Lens Cap Fidget Cover & Diamond Knurled Grip Bands:** Tactile textured knurling for thumb rolling.

## ⌚ Prototype 3: "Chrono-Winder"
**Inspiration:** Luxury Skeleton Tourbillon Diver Watch & Ratchet Bezel

![Chrono-Winder Prototype Sketch](C:/Users/chuda/.gemini/antigravity-ide/brain/5324f30b-bc16-4635-a890-31f0949b9622/prototype3_chrono_winder_1787158814381.jpg)

### 🎯 5+ Tactile Features
1. **Unidirectional 60-Click Rotating Bezel:** One-way rotating ring with internal dual-pawl leaf springs providing crisp diver-watch clicks.
2. **Push-Pull Crown Winder Knob:** Side knob with 2 positions (Push-in = free glide, Pull-out = engaging internal ratchet gear).
3. **Exposed Skeleton Planetary Gear Spinner:** Central open-face cluster with 1 sun gear driving 3 satellite planet gears for continuous gear spinning.
4. **Dual Spring Chronograph Click Pushers:** Top and bottom tactile plungers with distinct spring tension.
5. **Side Deployant Snap Clasp:** Hinged locking latch that snaps firmly against the chassis.
6. **Coin-Edge Tactile Perimeter:** High-relief milled grooves around the outer circumference.
